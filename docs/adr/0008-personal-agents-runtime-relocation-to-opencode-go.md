- **Status:** Accepted (2026-11-17 — certification complete: build gates 6/6, QA live matrix 7/7 on the laptop, remote runtime turn 1/1 on homesrv; gates measured with the relocated artifacts in place)
- **Date:** 2026-11-17 (proposed and accepted the same day)
- **Deciders:** User (Tony — economic mandate, one-invoice rule; executed the homesrv sync), Commander (orchestration + remote verification), go_architect (builder `#variant` emission proof), qa_architect (live E2E verification, resolved-model evidence), PE (binary parity verdict, service state), doc_architect (this record)
- **Evidence (laptop, 2026-11-17):** live verification — all 7 personal agents rc=0, Spanish answers, resolved `id`+`variant` read from the LIVE `session_message.data.model` table (NOT the zombie `message` table); `organiza` correctly shows variant ABSENT. Zero `provider.no-route`, zero HTTP 400/429, zero silent degradation to the account default. Compiled `agent/*.md` reload was picked up WITHOUT a daemon restart (config watcher confirmed live).

- **Sole data change:** `.config/opencode/builder/templates/model_profiles.json` lines 12-18. The 8 technical keys + `model` + `small_model` are unchanged (`opencode-go/qwen3.8-flash`). `agent/*.md` are generated artifacts — never hand-edited.

## Context

Three forces converged on 2026-11-17:

1. **The one-invoice rule.** Tony pays for **OpenCode Go Plus** (subscription). Until this change the 7 personal agents ran on the native `google` provider keyed by `GEMINI_API_KEY` — **pay-per-use on top of a subscription already paid for**. His stated position: *"no voy a usar una suscripcion que ya pago y otra que ya pago por uso"* ("I'm not going to use a subscription I already pay for AND another one I already pay per use"). Measured personal workload — ~1,002 input / 27,394 output tokens per turn — is worth ≈ **$0.75/month** in pay-per-use equivalent terms: economically irrelevant, so there is no cost objection to the move.

2. **The suspended Google account.** The Google account `andy.tech.castro@gmail.com` was suspended 2026-11-15 for a ToS violation (third-party use of the Antigravity login). The native API-key path (`generativelanguage.googleapis.com`) stayed legitimately operational — appeal unaffected by API-key traffic — but it kept the personal roster hostage to a **second billing surface** with its own failure modes: prepaid-balance **HTTP 402** hard stops, per-model **429** pools, transient **503** capacity events, and reasoning-token starvation of `maxOutputTokens` on hydrogen. One provider, one control plane, one invoice removes all of it.

3. **The parked Antigravity endgame.** If the account appeal succeeds, the original endgame was to serve the personal agents through `opencode-antigravity-auth` (subscription-backed Google OAuth, zero incremental cost). That path stays **parked**, not dead: the plugin's request route was broken upstream by the `systemInstruction` false-429 defect (PR #623 unmerged), and re-adopting it would mean re-opening the very ToS exposure the appeal promises to resolve. It must never be re-enabled without a new, explicit decision after reinstatement.

A secondary design constraint surfaced during option review: per-model ceilings on Go Plus are **asymmetric** and published as **($/month, requests/5h)** — `gemini-4-argon` 120/2260, `gpt-6-luna` **60**/16900, `mimo-v2.6-flash` 120/60200, `qwen4-max` 60/1850, `deepseek-v4.1-flash` 200/86700 — and residency/training gates are **account/workspace privacy settings invisible to the CLI and the builder**. The mapping below had to satisfy depth-per-agent, pool-sharing, and privacy posture simultaneously.

## Decision

Relocate all 7 personal agents onto the `opencode-go` gateway using the V2 `provider/model#variant` frontmatter syntax. Final mapping (verified live 2026-11-17):

| Agent | Before (2026-11-15 → 16) | After (2026-11-17) | Rationale |
|-------|--------------------------|---------------------|-----------|
| `finanzas` | `google/gemini-4-hydrogen` | `opencode-go/gemini-4-argon#medium` | Money data: flagship reasoning depth kept; `#medium` for the reasoning tier. Sensitive/reflective group unified on one model+variant so `finanzas`/`coach`/`life` share one pool and one behavior. |
| `coach` | `google/gemini-4-hydrogen` | `opencode-go/gemini-4-argon#medium` | Training/wellbeing reasoning — same treatment as `finanzas`. |
| `life` | `google/gemini-4-hydrogen` | `opencode-go/gemini-4-argon#medium` | Reflective journaling. **Unified to `#medium` with the other two rather than left at a lighter variant**: three agents carrying personal, long-form, emotionally-contextual conversations must not exhibit depth divergence between them, and a shared identical id+variant is one auditable pool with one rollback line. |
| `chef` | `google/gemini-3.8-flash` | `opencode-go/gpt-6-luna#medium` | Recipe generation benefits from `#medium` reasoning (multi-constraint substitutions); factual/lookup tier, not a privacy flagship. |
| `barista` | `google/gemini-3.8-flash` | `opencode-go/gpt-6-luna#none` | Machine-manual and coffee Q&A: shortest answer latency matters more than deliberation. **`#none` is a latency selector, NOT a cost lever** — gateway pricing is the same subscription allowance regardless of variant; killing thinking buys speed, not money. |
| `habitos` | `google/gemini-3.8-flash` | `opencode-go/gpt-6-luna#low` | Habit check-ins: trivially structured, `#low` suffices. Chosen over `deepseek-v4.1-flash` by the residency policy below. |
| `organiza` | `google/gemini-3.8-flash` | `opencode-go/mimo-v2.6-flash` **(bare, no suffix)** | Task/organizing lookups. **Carries NO `#variant` because `mimo-v2.6-flash` exposes no effort variants; any suffix (`#low`, `#none`, even `#medium`) yields `provider.no-route` at session start.** Verified: `organiza` resolves with variant field ABSENT, not null, not defaulted. This is the one row where the syntax must stay bare. |

Technical invariants locked with this decision:

1. **`#variant` survives the builder emission path.** Proven statically by go_architect and re-verified this session: `builder/main.go:125` regex `\{\{MODEL:([a-zA-Z0-9_]+)\}\}` constrains only the placeholder **key**; `:133` returns the profile **value verbatim**; `:188` writes raw bytes via `os.WriteFile`; `builder/go.mod` has **ZERO `require` entries** — no YAML library in the emission path at all. js-yaml 3.x (embedded in opencode 2.3.0) round-trips `model: opencode-go/gemini-4-argon#medium` intact, because the parser splits on the FIRST `/` and FIRST `#`.
2. **Two authoring traps (must never appear in templates or hand-written config):**
   - `model: x #medium` (space before `#`) → js-yaml reads `#` as a **COMMENT** and truncates the id to `x`.
   - `model: #medium` (nothing before `#`) → parses as **null** → silent substitution of the account default model.
3. **The builder has ZERO variant validation.** Failure modes, all silent at build time: unknown key → warning only and the literal `{{MODEL:x}}` survives into the artifact; empty value → YAML null → account default silently; **non-existent variant name → the build SUCCEEDS and every session of that agent dies at the first turn with `provider.no-route`.** `opencode models` prints ids WITHOUT variants, so variant validity cannot be checked statically — only a live turn proves it.
4. **Homesrv: version contract MEASURED and SATISFIED — synced 2026-11-17.** Pi is aarch64 running opencode **v2.2.5** (laptop 2.3.0) with NO builder — a pure artifact sink fed by `oc --sync` (agents leg: `rsync -avz --delete --backup`, pre-backup tar.gz in `~/backups/oc-sync/`; the run that shipped this state is `overwrites-agent-20261117-195248/`, 15 `.md` recoverable). PE measured both binaries directly: 2.2.5 and 2.3.0 resolve `model: opencode-go/gemini-4-argon#medium` **identically** — the `Variant unavailable` / `provider.no-route` / `activeVariant` code paths are present in both — so `#variant` is **SAFE_TO_SYNC** and the version axis of the `#` suffix is **not** a blocker. The contract remains as a rule for FUTURE suffixes: do not push an artifact to a node whose binary cannot parse it; verify parity (as was done here) before assuming.
5. **Rollback safety net retained.** `GEMINI_API_KEY` stays configured but **unused** — the documented fallback path back to `google/*` if the gateway mapping ever fails. It is NOT removed. The `experimental.policies` entry `{action: provider.use, resource: "google", effect: allow}` likewise stays, but is reclassified: after this move **nothing running consumes `google/*`**, so the rule is **no longer load-bearing** — retained only as the documented rollback path. (Not deleted: deleting it would close the door this ADR deliberately leaves open.)

## Consequences

**Positive:**
- **Google API spend → $0.** One invoice (OpenCode Go Plus). The HTTP 402 balance-exhaustion class of failure, tier-based rate-limit surprises, per-model Google pools, and AI Studio 503 capacity events are all gone from the running system.
- The **402 hard-stop guardrail is REPLACED** as the budget control by the gateway's **per-model ceilings** ($/month + requests/5h, counted over 5-hour, weekly AND monthly windows) plus a **burn detector**. Because the gateway `cost` field reads 0 client-side on both nodes, the detector is necessarily two-part: **request counts per model from `session_message` locally** (with the `session_id`/`sessionID` schema skew handled) and the **$/month consumption from the console / request-log export**, which is the only place the binding dollar ceiling is visible.
- Official FAQ posture: *"When you reach a Go limit, you can keep using the free models"* — `*-free` models (`beam-free`, `hy4-flash-free`, `longcat-2.5-free`) are the **overflow path**, unlimited while offered. "Extra Usage" (console-side credit) remains **OFF by default**, preserving the deliberate hard stop.
- `#variant` becomes a per-agent **latency/depth dial** instead of a cost lever: `#none`/`#low` for factual lookups, `#medium` for reasoning agents.
- Residency/training posture is now governed by ONE place: gateway account/workspace privacy settings ("Go blocks paid endpoints that train on your requests" by default), rather than Google API billing tier.

**Negative / accepted:**
- **The privacy counterparty changed.** Personal prompts/responses (finances, training logs, wellbeing journaling, household data) no longer flow to Google AI Studio; they flow through the **opencode-go gateway**. The accepted guarantee is the published default *"By default, Go blocks paid endpoints that train on your requests"* — an **account-level setting we cannot verify from the CLI or the builder**, and residency/training HTTP 400 gates are likewise **invisible to both**. Evidence that the gate exists and works: `opencode-go/deepseek-v4.1-flash` returned HTTP 400 *"This OpenCode Go model is hosted outside Europe and the United States. Select Global at .../settings/privacy#regions"*, and `iceman-contributor-free` returned HTTP 400 gated by the `#training` setting ("Allow paid endpoints that train on request data"). Tony toggled Global, tested it, then **REVOKED it and returned to EU/US** — so that 400 is the privacy control **WORKING, not a bug**. Consequence: deepseek is excluded and `habitos` went to `luna#low`. **Per-workspace residency also governs the sensitive agents (`finanzas`/`coach`/`life`)**: their data location is a workspace privacy setting, not a model choice.
- **3 of 7 agents share the luna pool** (`chef#medium`, `barista#none`, `habitos#low`) — one shared **$60/month** dollar ceiling (the lowest of the models this roster uses), while its request window (16,900/5h) is generous. The **sensitive trio shares `gemini-4-argon`'s 2,260 requests/5h window** — the tightest request-rate ceiling in the live roster; a burst of long reflective conversations is the realistic saturation case there. A heavy household burst can exhaust luna's monthly allowance while argon/mimo still have headroom; overflow lands on free models or a blocked turn.
- **Variant consumption is NOT auditable from the gateway console.** The request-log export shows `requestedModel`/`model` WITHOUT the `#variant`. It IS visible locally, but from one place only: `session_message.data.model.variant` (`opencode stats --models` gives per-model request/token counts — burn, not variant proof — and its `--cost` column reads 0). Corollaries: `reasoningTokens` is **NOT** valid variant evidence for argon (measured 0 at both low and medium); luna showed medium=41 vs low/none=0 (weak signal only); the `session_v2.model` column comes back **EMPTY — do not script against it.** **Schema skew between versions — load-bearing for any consumer script:** the `session_message` session column is **`session_id` on 2.2.5 (Pi)** and **`sessionID` on 2.3.0 (laptop)**; `session_v2`/`session_message` exist on both but with different key names (the Pi additionally lists `session_inbox` and `session_part`). A script that hard-codes one name is blind on the other node — accept both.
- **Local cost reporting is empty — the budget ceiling lives only in the console.** The gateway `cost` field reads **0 client-side on both nodes**: Go consumption is not exposed locally, only request counts. A local detector can therefore count **requests per model** from `session_message`, but the ceiling that actually binds this roster is the **$/month** figure, which exists only in the console / request-log export. The `opencode stats --models --cost` "burn detector" named above is thus a **request-side** instrument; the dollar side must be watched in the console (or via exported logs), never inferred from local numbers. Related measured data point: a trivial first-turn `finanzas` run on the Pi logged **58,700 input tokens with `cache.read = 0`** — expected on the FIRST turn of a new session (prompt caching requires an identical prior prefix), not a caching failure.
- **No automatic model fallback in V2** (unchanged platform law, now with sharper teeth): a 429/400 kills the agent turn; switching to a free model is **MANUAL**. With the Google pool gone, gateway ceilings are the only pools.
- **Homesrv runs the relocated roster — verified, and now observability-limited rather than blocked.** The Pi is aarch64 on **v2.2.5** (laptop 2.3.0) and PE measured that `#variant` resolution behaves identically on both binaries, so the sync went through (2026-11-17 19:52) and the remote runtime turn passed. Two measured consequences stay as accepted limitations:
  - **On 2.2.5 a broken agent frontmatter is INVISIBLE.** The `agent file skipped` log string does not exist in that binary (0 matches), so an agent whose `model:` value is eaten by the YAML-comment trap (`model: x #medium` → `model: null`) **silently disappears from the agent list with no warning at all**. `--print-logs` filtered by skip/frontmatter/invalid/error returns **empty output on both versions**, and 2.2.5 does not even have the `opencode run --archived` flag. Detection on the Pi is therefore limited to *counting agents* and *running a turn* — there is no diagnostic to read. On the laptop the same trap must be caught by inspecting the compiled `agent/*.md` and `session_message.data.model`, not by log mining.
  - **The Pi's opencode service is a systemd SYSTEM unit (`opencode`, active; restarted at sync time 19:52:52 CET by `oc --sync` → `ssh homesrv "sudo systemctl restart opencode"`).** Any doc claiming the Pi "has no systemd" or runs a user-session service is wrong.

- **ACCEPTED RISK — warm rollback credential (user decision, 2026-11-17, risk owner: Tony, not the platform).** The security gate offered three postures for `GEMINI_API_KEY` (keep as-is / stop exporting it to the daemon so rollback requires a deliberate action / keep the policy rule but delete the key). His answer: *"Dejarla como está, es la red de seguridad"* — keep it, it is the safety net. What that knowingly accepts: the key is present in the live daemon environment, the `{action: provider.use, resource: "google", effect: allow}` policy rule is still in force, and therefore the counterparty of any personal agent is **one flag away** — `opencode run --agent finanzas --model google/gemini-4-hydrogen` moves sensitive prompts to Google with **zero file edits, zero rebuild, a clean `git status`, no matching `tool.use` floor and no alarm**. The residual mitigation is doctrinal, not technical: the explicit authorization rule now recorded in `AGENTS.md` (rollback is a counterparty change requiring a user decision) and in the Migration/rollback bullet below. Second-order note, also accepted: if that key ever regressed to a free tier, the free tier **licenses submitted content for training** — the retired tier reasoning must not be treated as a live safety property.

- **ACCEPTED LIMITATION — no automated residency/training drift detector (user decision, 2026-11-17).** A canary was designed and then **cancelled by the user** before anything shipped ("Cancelarla, no aporta señal"). The probe would have requested `opencode-go/deepseek-v4.1-flash` and `opencode-go/iceman-contributor-free` and asserted both still fail with HTTP 400 — inverted logic, where a `200` would mean a privacy gate had been opened. It was rejected because it tests the **control mechanism, not the location of the data**: neither model is in the live roster, and `gemini-4-argon` / `gpt-6-luna` / `mimo-v2.6-flash` expose no gate to knock on, so a vendor-side hosting change would stay invisible to it — exactly as the workspace `Global` region flag itself was invisible to the CLI when it was flipped. It also had no trigger (the gate only opens if someone opens it) while its real cost was a permanent tool plus a timer plus maintenance. Accepted posture: manual coverage only — the two `AGENTS.md` troubleshooting rows that state a residency/training HTTP 400 is the privacy control **working** (never "fix" it with Global), plus a one-off manual probe (`opencode run -m opencode-go/deepseek-v4.1-flash`, expected: 400) whenever drift is suspected. Recorded so a future agent does not "discover" the gap and re-propose the same tool.

**Migration / rollback:**
- Rollback = revert `model_profiles.json` lines 12-18 to the `google/*` values, rebuild via the builder, verify `agent/*.md` frontmatter. Requires `GEMINI_API_KEY` present in the **daemon's process** environment (a rebuild alone never delivers env vars). ~2 min on the laptop; the `provider.use google` allow rule is retained precisely so this path is one-file, not three. **Cost correction (security gate 2026-11-17): the "~2 min, one file" framing describes only the git-visible shape.** `opencode run --agent finanzas --model google/gemini-4-hydrogen` produces the SAME counterparty switch with **zero file edits, zero rebuild, a clean `git status` and no policy floor matching it**, and the compiled routing artifact (`agent/*.md`) is itself gitignored — so nothing in version control records that the switch happened. **Authorization: switching a personal agent back to `google/*` is a user-authorized decision, never an agent action.** The retained allow rule makes the route *possible*; it does not make it *permitted*. On the Pi, whether the key is currently in the service environment is **NOT VERIFIED by this evidence set**: `systemctl show opencode -p Environment` returns **only `PATH`**, and that query does not display variables imported through `EnvironmentFile=` — so it proves nothing either way. Do not write "the Pi has no key"; a Pi-side rollback would need the key checked through the unit's `EnvironmentFile=`/drop-in, not via `-p Environment`.

## Verification (homesrv, 2026-11-17 — Commander-measured, post-sync)

The remote leg closed the last open question in this ADR. All four items are the Commander's own measurements on 2026-11-17 after the sync Tony executed; they supersede every earlier "pending" formulation in this document and in `AGENTS.md`.

## Alternatives considered

1. **Keep the native `google` provider (status quo ante).** Rejected by the user mandate: two simultaneous paid surfaces — a subscription already paid for plus pay-per-use — for a workload worth ≈$0.75/month on the gateway. Retains the 402/429/503 failure surface and the per-tier privacy reasoning that has to be re-verified against external docs every time it changes. (Retained as the **rollback path**, not as the running design.)
2. **`deepseek-v4.1-flash` for `habitos`** (cheap, high ceiling: 200/mo · 86,700/5h). Rejected by **residency policy**: measured HTTP 400 — hosted outside Europe/US; serving it requires switching the workspace privacy region to Global. Tony tested Global, revoked it, returned to EU/US. Non-negotiable: no household-agent data routed outside EU/US by an account setting we also rely on for the sensitive agents. `habitos` → `gpt-6-luna#low` instead.
3. **A `*-free` model (e.g. `beam-free`) for a household agent.** Rejected: free models are **unlimited but LIMITED-TIME** (the vendor says so: "while this model is free"). A permanent personal-infrastructure agent must not depend on a promotional tier whose end date is not ours to know; the vendor also warns free models "may come with a higher risk of prompts and responses being used for training". Free models are the documented **overflow path** only.
4. **Port the personal agents to the Antigravity plugin (subscription-backed OAuth, zero incremental cost).** **PARKED.** Blocked twice over: the Google account is suspended (ToS appeal pending), and the plugin's request path is broken upstream (`systemInstruction` false-429, PR #623 unmerged). Even post-reinstatement, re-adopting it re-opens the exact exposure the appeal commits not to repeat. Requires a new explicit user decision and a fresh plan; do not treat as a cleanup item.
